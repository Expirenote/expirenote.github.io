第一次使用，或想了解某個功能？
這些說明涵蓋最常用的操作與資料設定。

01掃描條碼，一定找得到商品嗎？

不一定。條碼辨識與商品資料查詢是兩個步驟；結果取決於已啟用資料庫是否收錄該商品。查不到時，可以拍攝包裝辨識文字或手動補齊。儲存時選擇記住條碼，之後便可在同一空間帶回你確認的資料。

02條碼能直接讀出有效期限嗎？

一般零售條碼不包含這件商品的批次有效期限。請核對包裝，使用拍照文字辨識或手動輸入日期；辨識結果也需要你確認。相同商品可保留不同日期的批次。

03物品櫃的照片與位置會同步嗎？

櫃體選擇、排列位置、編輯用照片副本與去背展示圖目前保存在這部裝置，不透過 App 的 iCloud 同步。關聯的普通商品照片屬於庫存資料，啟用 iCloud 後可隨庫存同步。從物品櫃移除物品不會刪除商品。

04需要 iCloud 或另外註冊帳號嗎？

不需要另外建立 App 帳號，核心庫存功能可以離線使用。iCloud 同步是選用功能；開啟後需有可用的 Apple 帳號、網路和 iCloud 空間。個人空間不會自動分享，家庭共享只涉及你選定的家庭空間。

05通知沒有出現，可以檢查什麼？

請先到 App 的「我的」確認提醒已開啟，再檢查 iPhone 設定中的通知權限、專注模式與通知摘要。位置提醒另需對應定位權限。通知送達及小工具更新時間由 iOS 管理，無法保證在指定時刻立即顯示。

06刪除商品後，為什麼還記得條碼？

本機條碼記憶與庫存分開保存。你可在「條碼商品資料 → 記住的商品資料」修改、刪除單筆，或清除目前空間的記憶。也可關閉「儲存時記住商品條碼」。

07如何備份資料？

在「我的 → 資料管理」選擇 JSON 或 CSV 匯出。JSON 可包含庫存、商品照片與待同步異動；CSV 提供商品與批次欄位。物品櫃排列、條碼記憶、商店及購物清單不包含在庫存匯出中。請妥善保存匯出檔案。

08App 會把照片傳給 AI，或顯示廣告嗎？

文字辨識與去背在裝置上處理，不傳送至外部 AI。若開啟 iCloud，普通商品照片會按照同步設定傳至 Apple。目前 1.0 正式版本設定關閉廣告；詳細資料處理方式請閱讀隱私權政策。

A good place to start

New to ExpireNote, or wondering how a feature works?
Here are the essentials, including how your data is handled.

01Will every barcode return product details?

No. Reading a barcode and finding its product record are separate steps. Results depend on the enabled databases. If a product is missing, you can recognize text from its packaging or enter details manually. Remembering the barcode when saving lets the same space reuse the details you confirmed.

02Can a barcode provide the expiry date?

An ordinary retail barcode does not contain the batch expiry date of the item in your hand. Check the packaging and use photo text recognition or manual entry. Recognized dates still need your confirmation. The same product can have separate batches with different dates.

03Are cabinet photos and positions synchronized?

Cabinet choices, placement, editing image copies and background-removed display images currently stay in this device’s app storage and do not use app iCloud sync. An associated ordinary item photo belongs to inventory and can sync when iCloud is enabled. Removing an item from a cabinet does not delete the inventory item.

04Do I need iCloud or a separate account?

No separate app account is required, and core inventory features work offline. iCloud sync is optional and requires an available Apple Account, network and iCloud storage. Your personal space is not automatically shared; household sharing applies only to the space you choose.

05What should I check if reminders do not appear?

Check that reminders are enabled under My, then review notification permission, Focus and Scheduled Summary in iPhone Settings. Location reminders need their corresponding location permissions. iOS controls notification delivery and widget refresh timing, so exact delivery times are not guaranteed.

06Why is a barcode remembered after deleting an item?

Remembered barcodes are stored separately from inventory. Under Barcode product data → Remembered product data, you can edit or delete entries, or clear the current space’s memory. You can also turn off remembering a barcode when saving.

07How can I back up my inventory?

Under My → Data management, export JSON or CSV. JSON can include inventory, ordinary item photos and pending changes; CSV contains item and batch fields. Cabinet layouts, barcode memory, stores and shopping lists are not part of inventory exports. Keep exported files somewhere safe.

08Are photos sent to AI services, or are ads shown?

Text recognition and background removal run on your device without an external AI service. If you enable iCloud, ordinary item photos follow your sync settings and are sent to Apple. Advertising is disabled in the current production configuration of version 1.0. See the privacy policy for full details.
